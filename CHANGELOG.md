# Changelog

What changed, written for the family first. Newest at the top.
Every change adds a line under *Unreleased* in the same commit ([CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
When a batch has been trialled, its lines move under a dated heading.

## Unreleased
- **Ping is a word, not a pin** (Mike, 28 Nov, by voice): the button at the top of a run now says **Ping**, and its cards are called vehicles (a driver, or a driver plus helpers). Same feature as Where's everyone? below.
- **Where's everyone?** (Mike, 28 Nov, by voice): while on duty, tap 📍 at the top of a run to ping everyone else on duty at once. Each car comes back as one card, driver and helper combined: where they are now and how far from you (with a Google Maps button), the stops they've just done, and where they're heading next. Same rules as Dispatch's Ping: everyone is told, and nothing is shared off duty. So we can coordinate, and Life360 can go on battery-saving settings. [Decision 0072](docs/decisions/0072-wheres-everyone.md).
- **Voice-to-Laptop Bridge** (Mike, 26 Nov): any Claude session, including voice on the drive, can now see whether Mike's home laptop is on and hand it work in a queue of its own. The laptop picks a job up within seconds, does one at a time, and reports back; anything iffy waits for Mike's Allow, and there's a kill switch. STB changes still go through voice builds. Nothing changes on the phones. See [decision 0070](docs/decisions/0070-laptop-bridge.md).
- **An address that comes back lands in its usual place** (Mike, 23 Nov, after 26 Marine Parade on Run 6): if your own order was made while an address was off the sheet, it now goes where the usual order has it, not beside whatever it follows on the sheet, and Stan asks about any address your order has never placed that sits away from its street. [Decision 0068](docs/decisions/0068-returning-addresses-and-one-letterbox.md), lesson L-013.
- **One letterbox, two tiles** (Mike, 23 Nov, 12 Selwyn Road): Stan spots the same letterbox keyed two ways and asks a dispatcher whether to show one tile; from the next sheet it is one tile with both papers.

- **Stan's report card now tests the driving** (Mike, 23 Nov): the test Stan has to pass is on the drives between stops, the only part of a morning an order can change. The whole-morning figures stay for comparison. **He doesn't pass this one either** (about 16% out, target 5%): the time between two ticks is mostly what happens at the letterbox, not the drive. See [decision 0067](docs/decisions/0067-gate-on-the-driving.md).
- **Stan's report card on Insights** (Mike, 23 Nov): Insights now shows how well Stan can predict how long a morning takes, by testing each past morning against the other days: his typical miss, how many were within 5, 10 and 20 per cent, and a line per run. He has to pass it before he's allowed to suggest a quicker order. **He doesn't pass yet** (about 20% out, target 5%), so he isn't suggesting anything.
