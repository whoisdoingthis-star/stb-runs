# 0065. Stan's School and Sandbox Stan: training that can't go out of date

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context
Mike (23 Nov 2026): "I'd like to build a site that Stan teaches new drivers and existing drivers use, and also to point existing drivers to new functionality, similar to what we have done for Ollie. That way training can be updated where adjustments or reconfigurations are made. Email alerts to all relevant users pushed to alert them to the new functionality, and even have a Sandbox Stan app for them to practise using the new functionality. This feels like a better solution than videos, which become stale as soon as a change is made." He agreed the plan the same morning, with three choices: a new driver can't drive until trained; the site and sandbox are open (made-up data only) while progress needs a sign-in; and one email at the end of the day rather than one per publish.

## Decision
1. **Stan's School** (`learn/`, on the same site as the app) is built from the app's own code. Courses are lists in `js/training.js` whose steps are **What's new cards** (`js/whatsnew.js`): same words, same why, and Stan reads each with the clip already recorded for the card. A course can add a few steps of its own (a welcome, how to practise); their spoken lines are Stan lines (`learn.<course>.<step>`) and are recorded on publish like any other ([0061](0061-stan-records-himself.md)). Four courses: New driver, New helper, Getting more from Stan, and Dispatch.
2. **It can't fall behind.** `test/training.mjs` fails a publish when a card isn't taught in any course, when a course teaches a card meant for other people, or when a card marked `replaced` is still taught. So a change that gets a card (rule 8 of CONTRIBUTING) gets a lesson in the same commit, and a card that stops being true has to be retired from the courses.
3. **Pictures of the real app.** A card can name a picture (`shot`). `tools/training-shots.py` takes them in Sandbox Stan on a fixed morning with motion off, and `.github/workflows/training-shots.yml` takes them again after each publish that changes the app and saves any that changed. A lesson can't name a picture the job doesn't take (also in `test/training.mjs`).
4. **Sandbox Stan** (`sandbox/`) is the real app (`js/app.js`) in demo mode, whatever `config.js` says, with two made-up practice runs (`js/practice-data.js`: invented streets, one of each thing worth meeting: a shop drop, a second paper, two at one box, a new start, a complaint, a note). Everything it saves goes into its own corner of the phone's storage (`sbx:` in front of every name), so practice can never touch the real app's runs, duty or odometer on the same phone. If a browser won't allow that, the sandbox doesn't start. A practice bar on top gives one task at a time.
5. **Practice is checked, not claimed.** Each task (`TASKS` in `js/training.js`) is ticked off by reading what the practice app actually saved (an On duty with the crew answered and the odometer in; three ticks; a couldn't-deliver; off duty with the end odometer). There's no "I did it" button.
6. **Progress** is kept on the phone first and sent when signed in (`training_progress`, one row per finished course or task, only ever added). The school, the sandbox and the app share the site's sign-in, so nobody signs in twice. The People page on Dispatch shows each person's training.
7. **New drivers train first** (Mike, 23 Nov 2026). `members.needs_training` is on for people added from today and off for everyone already on the list. A driver with it on gets "Driver training first" when they tap On duty, with a button to the school, and can still **come along as a helper** that day (they're then a helper for the shift: no odometer asked). It goes away when the driver course and its four practice tasks are done. A dispatcher can switch it off on People for someone who already knows the job.
8. **The end-of-day email** (`tools/training-digest.mjs`, every evening at 8:23pm NZ daylight time): each person gets the cards for them from the last week that they haven't read in the app, each with a **Learn it with Stan** link to its lesson, and a link to Sandbox Stan. A card is never emailed twice (`training_emails`). It sends through Resend, the same service Ollie uses.

## Consequences
- Writing a card now also means choosing its course. That's one line in `js/training.js`; the check says which card is missing.
- Card wording is written as a change ("the Skip button is gone"), and is now also read by people who never saw the old way. Good enough for now; where it reads badly, a course can teach it with a step of its own that `covers` the card.
- The pictures job commits to `main` by itself. It only does so when a picture really changed.
- **Email needs Mike (not done yet):** Resend only delivers to the account owner until a domain is verified. Until `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` are in the repository's secrets, the evening job is a dry run that lists who would get what. Steps are in the [runbook](../runbook.md#stans-end-of-day-email).
- The gate is in the app, like the other hard lines ([0057](0057-duty-hard-lines.md)); the database doesn't refuse an On duty. Good enough for a family team; a contractor team would want it on the server too.
- A driver signed in on a phone with no signal and no saved status isn't gated that once (the app can't know). Their first sign-in always has signal.
- Recording the school's own lines cost about 1,500 characters of Stan's voice; the card steps reuse clips already made.
- Checked by `test/training.mjs` and, in a browser, `test/e2e-school.py`.
