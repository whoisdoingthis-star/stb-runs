# 0021. Stan in the app: the face of help and good news, used sparingly

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context
Stan, the little robot from the family video (an original design in the STB colours), went down well. Mike (12 Nov 2026) asked to add him to the app and suggested Stan as the button for navigation help. The app is used at 5am, one-handed, and Mum uses big text: a mascot that chats all the time would get in the way.

## Decision
- **Stan is the Driver Assistant.** His head is the button in the bottom bar, labelled Stan. In the Assistant panel his mouth lights and a word say how he's guiding: blue *Turn by turn*, amber *Pointing the way*, grey *Waiting for GPS*. His lights blink and his antenna pin pulses while the phone speaks, and the voice is a touch higher while Stan is on. The arrow and distance stay the biggest things in the panel.
- **He turns up for good news and first-time help:** a hop in "✓ Run 11 done. On to Run 6", a wave for On the run and Off duty, and a hello on the On duty card the first time someone sees it (gone after their first shift).
- **He stays out of** tiles, ticks, the run list and the opening animation, and never carries meaning on his own: every Stan comes with words.
- **One file** (`js/stan.js`, inline SVG, a few poses). He follows night mode and big text, keeps still when the phone asks for reduced motion, and **⚙ Settings → Stan: Show / Hide** turns him off (back to the plain Assistant button).
- Next step (not in this change): Stan on empty screens ("All done", "Nothing waiting") and in Dispatch's empty states and tips.

Not chosen: Stan replacing the opening animation (the pin becoming the logo is the brand moment); a talking help character that answers questions (he can't, and shouldn't look as if he can).

## Consequences
The button's accessible name is "Stan, the Driver Assistant". Phones' voices differ, so the higher pitch sounds younger on some phones more than others.
