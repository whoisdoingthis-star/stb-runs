# 0008. Delivery photos for disputed complaints

- **Date:** 2026-11-08
- **Status:** Accepted

## Context
Customers sometimes report non-delivery when the paper was delivered. The family photographs later deliveries and sends them on WhatsApp.

## Decision
A camera button on complaint and watched tiles, and after any tick. Photos are stamped with address, time, driver and GPS, stored privately and recorded against the address, and shared through the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp). Not a double tap: a single tap already ticks, and hold is used for moving tiles.

## Consequences
Evidence that explains itself when forwarded. Needs signal to save to the run; sharing works without it.
