# Family guide

How to use the STB Runs app on a delivery morning. No tech needed.

## First time on a phone
1. Open **whoisdoingthis-star.github.io/stb-runs** in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Add it to your home screen: Chrome **⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install**; Safari **Share → Add to Home Screen**. The icon is the yellow **STB Together** logo.
4. Allow location when it asks. It records where each delivery was made, which helps us put the run in a better order later.

iPhone: links opened from Messages open in Safari, which keeps its own sign-in, so sign in once in Safari too.

## Night mode, big text and layout
- The app is **dark (Night) by default**, easy on the eyes before sunrise. Change it in **⚙ Settings** on the Runs page, or **⋯** inside a run: **Night**, **Day**, or **Auto** (dark from 7pm to 7am).
- **Text: Big** makes everything larger, with one address per row, and finished addresses turn solid green with a big tick and **DONE**. It is on automatically for Mum.
- **Tiles: Two across** (read left to right, then down) or **One per row**.
These are remembered on that phone only.

## The Runs page
- Each run is a big card: **Run 6**, how many stops, items (papers and magazines) and dockets. Tap it to **Start** (or **Continue**).
- **Pick** on each card is what to collect at the depot, from the report: e.g. **NZH** 1 bundle of 140 + 5 loose (includes 15 for retail), **WD** 2 loose. Shop copies are counted in with the NZH; the note says how many are for shops.
- **Open all** opens every run for that day together.
- The line under the date says when the sheets were last updated, and whether anything changed since the previous sheet ("Run 13: 2 details changed"). Tap **What changed on Run 13** to see the list.
- **To collect** (tap to open): how many of each paper or magazine every run needs, how many **delivery dockets** to bring (one for each shop), and the **depot pick** for every run in one list, with an **All runs** line at the bottom: the day's total for each paper or magazine as whole bundles plus loose, worked out from that day's bundle size ("NZH 3 bundles of 80 + 79 loose = 319"). The bundle size comes from the day's sheets, as it changes from day to day.
- **The opening**: every time you open the app or come back to it (and when you sign in), a run line draws across the screen, three stops tick green, each with a little twinkle, and a pin drops and becomes the STB logo. It takes about a second; tap to skip it. It is **silent** unless you choose **⚙ Settings → Opening → Chimes** on your own phone: then each stop and the pin plays its own soft note, going up. It never plays while you are inside a run, so coming back from Maps or the camera doesn't get in the way of ticking. **Play the opening** in Settings shows it any time. On iPhones the chimes may only sound after you have touched the screen.
- **⚙ Settings**: text size, colours, tiles, the opening's sound, **Get Runs from RouteSmart** (use it if a change is phoned in), **Upload a run sheet**, change password, sign out.
- Two tabs at the top: **Runs to do** shows only today's and upcoming runs that aren't finished. **History** has every finished run (all stops ticked, or closed by Mike after a paper run) and earlier days. A run moves to History by itself once it's done, so the screen stays clean. You can still open a run from History.

## Doing a run
If you do a run on the **paper sheet**, that's fine: Mike closes it in Dispatch afterwards and it shows on the Runs page as **✓ Closed, done on paper by** you. You can still open it.

- The **purple NEXT tile** is your next stop. Tap it when delivered: it turns green with your name and the time, and the next one lights up (the list scrolls to it if it's off screen).
- The **bar at the bottom** shows what you just ticked and the next stop in full, on two lines, with the buttons underneath: **Undo**, **⚠** (problem), the **camera**, the **arrow** (directions), and **Next ↓** (jumps to the next stop). It stays until your next tick, so there's no rush.
- A ticked tile shows who ticked it and when beside its **✓**, and stays the same size, so the tiles below don't move as you tick.
- **Driver Assistant:** tap the **arrow** (it says **Assistant** before your first tick). A panel opens above the bottom bar and **talks you to the next stop**: the street to turn into, how far, which side the letterbox is on, and anything on the sheet you need to know ("2 papers", "put it in the Herald box"). It moves on by itself when you tick, and never ticks for you. 🔊 turns the voice off or on, **✕** closes it. It switches itself on the first time you do a run you haven't done before. On iPhone, tap the screen once so it's allowed to talk.
- **Google Maps:** tap **Maps** in the Assistant panel and Google Maps opens with directions to the **next stop** only. For the village it goes to where you park, not each letterbox. On Android, switch back to the run and Google Maps keeps going in a small floating window; on iPhone, swap between the two apps. Only use it with the phone in its holder.
- **Couldn't deliver, or not enough papers?** Tick the address as normal, then tap **⚠** in the bottom bar and pick the reason (gate locked, dog, no letterbox, door code didn't work, not safe, ran out) or how many you left. **Other reason…** lets you type your own. The tile turns **red** (not delivered) or **amber** (short) and Dispatch sees it straight away. Changed your mind? Tap ⚠ again and choose *It was delivered after all*, or Undo.
- **Street names** are banners in capitals with a count (0/5); the banner turns green when the street is done. The banner of the street you are in **stays at the top** as you scroll, so you always know which street the numbers belong to.
- The run's date is beside its name at the top. **Unit** and **Suite** numbers are in bold white (black in Day colours) under the house number.
- The top shows the run, the date, small status dots (GPS, whether ticks have been sent, who else is on the run), progress, and a clock from the first tick. Tap the progress line to jump back to the top.
- The **magnifying glass** finds a number, street or note. **⋯** has everything else: To do only, your order or the sheet's, tiles, text, colours, text a link to the run, door codes, refresh, sheet checks, and (in red, at the bottom) putting the run back in sheet order or unticking everything.
- Unit numbers read the way you'd say them: **12** with **Unit 2C** underneath, **87** with **Suite 1**.

### Tile colours
| Looks like | Means |
|---|---|
| Plain | Herald (NZH) only |
| **Blue**, blue chip **NZH + DOCKET** | A shop. Leave the papers **and a delivery docket** |
| **Yellow**, yellow chip | A different paper (WD, LIS, SST, TV Guide…) |
| **Orange**, dark orange chips | More than one paper at this address |
| **Purple outline, NEXT** | Your next stop |
| **Green** | Delivered |
| Red **COMPLAINT** with a line underneath | A complaint, e.g. "Missed delivery on Sat 7 Nov". Take extra care, and a photo |
| Notes on the tile | The customer's delivery instructions (up to 3 lines in two-across; one per row shows all) |

### Photos (for complaints)
When a customer says they didn't get their paper but we delivered it, a photo settles it.
- Complaint addresses, and any address we have asked to watch, have a **📷 Take photo** button on the tile.
- For any other address, tick it, then tap the **camera** in the bottom bar.
- The camera opens. The photo is stamped with the address, date, time and who delivered it, saved to the run, and the tile shows **📷 Photo taken**. Taking the photo also ticks the delivery.
- Tap **Send (WhatsApp…)** and choose the run owner in WhatsApp.
- **Took it with your phone's own camera, or forgot to tap the camera straight after ticking?** In the run, tap **⋯ → 📷 Add a photo to an address…**, type the number or street (e.g. *27 Luplau*), pick the address, then **Choose from my photos** (or **Take a photo now**). It's saved to that address, marked *added later*, keeps the time the photo was taken, and doesn't tick anything.
- Tick **Ask for a photo at this address every delivery** to keep the camera button on that address every day, for everyone, until someone unticks it.

### Working a run together
Anyone who opens the same run (for example from a texted link) sees the same list. Ticks show up on every phone within a second or two, and the top bar says who did what. "With Lisa" shows who else has it open.

### No signal?
Keep going. Ticks are saved on the phone and sent when there is signal ("3 to send" at the top). Photos need signal to save to the run; if one can't, it says so and lets you try again (you can still send it on WhatsApp).

## Changing the order of a run
If the sheet's order is wrong, **press and hold** a tile for half a second until it lifts, drag it, and let go. A blue line shows where it will go (left half of a tile = before it, right half = after). Hold a **street name** to move the whole street.
To move something a long way, keep holding it and drag it towards the **top or bottom of the screen**: the list scrolls by itself, faster the closer you are to the edge and the longer you hold it there. Move away from the edge to stop, then drop it in place.
**Everyone can have their own order.** When Lisa or Mum moves tiles, it's saved as **their own order** for that run, used every time *they* open it, and Mike's order (the usual one) isn't touched. The switch under the header then says **↕ My order** / **📄 Sheet order**. **⋯ → Use the usual order again** drops your own order. When Mike (dispatcher) moves tiles, he changes the usual order everyone else sees, and only he has **⋯ → Put back in the sheet's order…**. The sheet's original order is always kept, and switching only changes what your phone shows.
Run 8 keeps its fixed building order and can't be rearranged.

## Your password
**⚙ Settings → Change password** on the Runs page. If you forget it, ask Mike for a temporary one.

## New version of the app
The app updates itself. If it says "A new version is ready", tap **Reload**. If something looks old, close the app fully and open it again.
