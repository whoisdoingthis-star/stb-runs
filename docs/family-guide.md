# Family guide

How to use the STB Runs app on a delivery morning. No tech needed.

## First time on a phone
1. Open **whoisdoingthis-star.github.io/stb-runs** in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Add it to your home screen: Chrome **⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install**; Safari **Share → Add to Home Screen**. The icon is **Stan's face** on black (new 14 Nov; if you installed it earlier, the old yellow logo may stay until you remove the app from your home screen and add it again).
4. Allow location when it asks. It records where each delivery was made, which helps us put the run in a better order later.

iPhone: links opened from Messages open in Safari, which keeps its own sign-in, so sign in once in Safari too.

## Night mode, big text and layout
- The app is **dark (Night) by default**, easy on the eyes before sunrise. Change it in **⚙ Settings** on the Runs page, or **⋯** inside a run: **Night**, **Day**, or **Auto** (dark from 7pm to 7am).
- **Text: Big** makes everything larger, with one address per row, and finished addresses turn solid green with a big tick and **DONE**. It is on automatically for Mum.
- **Tiles: Two across** (read left to right, then down) or **One per row**.
These are remembered on that phone only.

## The Runs page
- Each run is a big card: **Run 6**, how many stops, items (papers and magazines) and dockets. Tap it to **Start** (or **Continue**).
- **Pick** on each card is what to collect at the depot, from the report: e.g. **NZH** 1 bundle of 140 + 5 loose (includes 15 for retail), **WD** 2 loose. Shop copies are counted in with the NZH; the note says how many are for shops.
- **Open all** opens every run for that day together.
- The line under the date says when the sheets were last updated, and whether anything changed since the previous sheet ("Run 13: 2 details changed"). Tap **What changed on Run 13** to see the list.
- **To collect** (tap to open): how many of each paper or magazine every run needs, how many **delivery dockets** to bring (one for each shop), and the **depot pick** for every run in one list, with an **All runs** line at the bottom: the day's total for each paper or magazine as whole bundles plus loose, worked out from that day's bundle size ("NZH 3 bundles of 80 + 79 loose = 319"). The bundle size comes from the day's sheets, as it changes from day to day.
- **The opening**: every time you open the app or come back to it (and when you sign in), Stan pops up with **Send The Boy** written on him, winks, does a spin and cheers. It takes about a second; tap to skip it. Each of his moves plays its own soft note, going up. For a silent opening, choose **⚙ Settings → Opening → Silent** on your own phone. It never plays while you are inside a run, so coming back from Maps or the camera doesn't get in the way of ticking. **Play the opening** in Settings shows it any time. On iPhones the chimes may only sound after you have touched the screen.
- **⚙ Settings**: text size, colours, tiles, the opening's sound, **Get Runs from RouteSmart** (use it if a change is phoned in), **Upload a run sheet**, change password, sign out.
- Two tabs at the top: **Runs to do** shows only today's and upcoming runs that aren't finished. **History** has every finished run (all stops ticked, or closed by Mike after a paper run) and earlier days. A run moves to History by itself once it's done, so the screen stays clean. You can still open a run from History.

### On duty and My runs
- When you get to the depot, tap **On duty** (the big button at the top of the Runs page). A clock starts: it counts sorting and loading too. If the phone knows you're at the depot it says so.
- It changes to **On the run** by itself when you drive off, or when you tick your first stop.
- **Choose my runs**: tap your runs in the order you'll do them (a number appears on each), then **Start my runs**. When you finish one, the app says "✓ Run 11 done. On to Run 6" and carries on with the next.
- Helping someone else finish theirs? Just open their run and tick. When it's done, the app offers **Go to** your next run.
- The bar at the top of a run shows **finish about 6:35 am**: about when all the runs you have open will be done, at the pace you're going. If that's later than you'd like, ring someone for a hand. It also shows on the On duty card.
- At the end, tap **Off duty**. After your last run the app asks if you're finished. **Forgot?** Once all of the day's runs are finished (every stop ticked, or the run closed), the app puts you off duty by itself, from the time the last run finished, and Stan tells you next time you open it.

### Stan's plan for the day
When Mike or Lisa has made a plan in Dispatch, **Stan pops up once** the next time you open the app and tells you:
- who you're with (you're driving with a helper, on your own, or you're helping someone);
- when to be **at the depot**, when the **first paper** should go out, and when you should be **all done**;
- your runs, in order.

Tap **Got it**. The plan stays on the Runs page in a yellow box; tap it to see it again. On the morning, **My runs** is already filled in in that order, so you just tap **Continue**.

### Going on duty: who's with you?
**Drivers:** tap **On duty** and the app asks who is helping you.
1. Tap each helper's name. Someone not on the list? Tap **Other…**, type their name and tap **Add**; they'll be a button next time. On your own? Tap **Just me**. You can't carry on until you've picked.
2. Then tap **Prepping at the depot** (still folding, bagging or loading) or **Prepped: starting the run** (ready to drive off).
3. If you were prepping, tap **Leaving the depot now** when you drive off. (If you forget, the app works it out when the car moves or you tick your first stop.)

**Helpers:** tap **On duty**, then tap **who's driving**. That's it.

### Spare papers
Drivers only. Both questions have a **Skip** button, so they never hold you up.
- **When you leave the depot,** Stan asks **Spares on board?** Tap **+** for each spare paper you have (a rough count is fine), then **Save**.
- **When you go off duty,** Stan asks **Spares left?** and whether any went to someone. Tap **+** next to their name, or **Someone else…** to add a name. Then **Save**.
- On Sundays there's a second row for the Sunday Star-Times.
- **The odometer:** the same two sheets ask for the big kilometres number on the dash. Type it in if you can; leave it blank if you can't. It's how we know how far the morning really was.

### Stan talks
Stan now speaks at the big moments: when you go on duty (he'll say good morning by name), when you leave the depot, when a run or the whole morning is done, when there's a note for you, and a few more. The words are always on the screen too. He says different things on different days, and he doesn't comment on everything.
- **Too chatty, or the house is asleep?** Tap the cog, then **Stan talks → Quiet**. His words still show.
- He needs one tap on the screen before he can make a sound, so he's silent when the app first opens.
- The Driver Assistant's directions are still in his old voice.

### Your morning
When you go off duty, Stan shows you how the morning went (if you forget, he shows you the next time you open the app):
- **The big line at the top:** when your last paper went out, and how long before the deadline (or after it).
- **The boxes:** time at the depot, time on the run, stops and papers, **km on the runs** (worked out from where you ticked) and **km on the clock** (from your odometer readings).
- **Pace:** how many minutes quicker or slower you were than **your own usual** for the same runs, and a little graph of your last mornings. Above the dotted line is quicker; the big yellow dot is today. You're only ever compared with yourself.
- **Quality:** every stop ticked; how many ticks were made at the address (tick as you drop the paper, not a few houses on); and any ticks with no location, which means location needs switching on for the app.

**Helpers:** you see the numbers of the driver you were with that day, and your graph follows whoever you helped each day.

Tap **Done**. The **📈 Your morning** button on the Runs page shows it again that day.

### The notice board
The **📌 Notice board** button on the Runs page is for telling the family something: "papers will be late", "the gate code has changed".
- **To leave a note:** Notice board → **Leave a note**. Type it, choose **Everyone** or the people it's for, choose **For 3 days** or **Until I remove it**, then **Post it**.
- **When someone has left you a note,** Stan pops up with it when you next open the app. Tap **Got it** so they know you've seen it.
- The board shows who has seen each note and who hasn't yet. You can take down your own notes.
- **A rule everyone should keep following?** Choose **📌 Pin as a rule** when you post it. It stays at the top of the board under **How we do things**.
- The board also shows **This morning**: when each run finished and the spares. The app works that out, so you don't need to write it.
- It only shows when the app is opened, so **ring or text for anything urgent**.

## Doing a run
If you do a run on the **paper sheet**, that's fine: Mike closes it in Dispatch afterwards and it shows on the Runs page as **✓ Closed, done on paper by** you. You can still open it.

- The **purple NEXT tile** is your next stop. Tap it when delivered: it turns green with your name and the time, and the next one lights up (the list scrolls to it if it's off screen).
- The **bar at the bottom** shows what you just ticked and the next stop in full, on two lines, with the buttons underneath: **Undo**, **⚠** (problem), the **camera**, the **arrow** (directions), and **Next ↓** (jumps to the next stop). It stays until your next tick, so there's no rush.
- A ticked tile shows who ticked it and when beside its **✓**, and stays the same size, so the tiles below don't move as you tick.
- **Driver Assistant:** tap **Stan** (the little robot) in the bottom bar. A panel opens above the bottom bar and **talks you to the next stop**: the street to turn into, how far, which side the letterbox is on, and anything on the sheet you need to know ("2 papers", "put it in the Herald box"). It moves on by itself when you tick, and never ticks for you. 🔊 turns the voice off or on, **✕** closes it. It switches itself on the first time you do a run you haven't done before. On iPhone, tap the screen once so it's allowed to talk.
- **Google Maps:** tap **Maps** in the Assistant panel and Google Maps opens with directions to the **next stop** only. For the village it goes to where you park, not each letterbox. On Android, switch back to the run and Google Maps keeps going in a small floating window; on iPhone, swap between the two apps. Only use it with the phone in its holder.
- **Tapped a green tile?** If you only just ticked it, tapping again unticks it (or use **Undo**). If it was ticked a while ago, or by someone else, the app asks first: **✓ Delivered just now** keeps it ticked with the time and place from now (use this if you're at the letterbox and it was already green), **Untick it** if it hasn't been delivered, or **Leave it as it is**.
- **Couldn't deliver, or not enough papers?** Tick the address as normal, then tap **⚠** in the bottom bar and pick the reason (gate locked, dog, no letterbox, door code didn't work, not safe, ran out) or how many you left. **Other reason…** lets you type your own. The tile turns **red** (not delivered) or **amber** (short) and Dispatch sees it straight away. Changed your mind? Tap ⚠ again and choose *It was delivered after all*, or Undo.
- **Street names** are banners in capitals with a count (0/5); the banner turns green when the street is done. The banner of the street you are in **stays at the top** as you scroll, so you always know which street the numbers belong to.
- The run's date is beside its name at the top. **Unit** and **Suite** numbers are in bold white (black in Day colours) under the house number.
- The top shows the run, the date, small status dots (GPS, whether ticks have been sent, who else is on the run), progress, and a clock from the first tick. Tap the progress line to jump back to the top.
- The **magnifying glass** finds a number, street or note. **⋯** has everything else: To do only, your order or the sheet's, tiles, text, colours, text a link to the run, door codes, refresh, sheet checks, and (in red, at the bottom) putting the run back in sheet order or unticking everything.
- Unit numbers read the way you'd say them: **12** with **Unit 2C** underneath, **87** with **Suite 1**.

### Tile colours
| Looks like | Means |
|---|---|
| Plain | Herald (NZH) only |
| **Blue**, blue chip **NZH + DOCKET** | A shop. Leave the papers **and a delivery docket** |
| **Yellow**, yellow chip | A different paper (WD, LIS, SST, TV Guide…) |
| **Orange**, dark orange chips | More than one paper at this address |
| **Purple outline, NEXT** | Your next stop |
| **Green** | Delivered |
| Red **COMPLAINT** with a line underneath | A complaint, e.g. "Missed delivery on Sat 7 Nov". Take extra care, and a photo |
| Notes on the tile | The customer's delivery instructions (up to 3 lines in two-across; one per row shows all) |

### Photos (for complaints)
When a customer says they didn't get their paper but we delivered it, a photo settles it.
- Complaint addresses, and any address we have asked to watch, have a **📷 Take photo** button on the tile.
- For any other address, tick it, then tap the **camera** in the bottom bar.
- The camera opens. The photo is stamped with the address, date, time and who delivered it, saved to the run, and the tile shows **📷 Photo taken**. Taking the photo also ticks the delivery.
- Tap **Send (WhatsApp…)** and choose the run owner in WhatsApp.
- **Took it with your phone's own camera, or forgot to tap the camera straight after ticking?** In the run, tap **⋯ → 📷 Add a photo to an address…**, type the number or street (e.g. *27 Luplau*), pick the address, then **Choose from my photos** (or **Take a photo now**). It's saved to that address and marked *added later*. A photo **chosen from the phone that took it** keeps the real time it was taken, and **if the stop isn't ticked yet, the delivery is recorded at that time** (handy if a phone dies mid-run and someone else takes the photos). Add it from the phone that took it: a picture sent through WhatsApp loses its time, so it's saved but ticks nothing, and the app tells you.
- Tick **Ask for a photo at this address every delivery** to keep the camera button on that address every day, for everyone, until someone unticks it.

### Working a run together
Anyone who opens the same run (for example from a texted link) sees the same list. Ticks show up on every phone within a second or two, and the top bar says who did what. "With Lisa" shows who else has it open.

### No signal?
Keep going. Ticks are saved on the phone and sent when there is signal ("3 to send" at the top). Photos need signal to save to the run; if one can't, it says so and lets you try again (you can still send it on WhatsApp).

## New customers get a photo
A tile marked **New** (a new customer's first paper) shows **📷 Take photo**. Please take one: it proves the first delivery was made.

## Closing a pop-up
Every pop-up has a **✕** at its top right. It stays there even when you scroll down a long one, so you never have to hunt for it. Tapping the dark area above the pop-up closes it too.

## If Dispatch pings you
While you're **on duty**, Mike can tap **Ping** in Dispatch to see where you are right now (handy if you need a hand or a run is running late). Your phone answers once, and you'll always see a message: "Mike checked where you are." It only works while the app is open, and never when you're off duty.

## Your icon
**⚙ Settings → My icon → Change**: pick one of the little robots, or tap **Upload a photo** to use a picture of yourself (it's cropped to a small circle). It shows beside your name, and Mike sees it in Dispatch. Only you can change yours.

## Photos and problems
The bottom bar of a run always has **Problem** (⚠) and **Photo** (📷). They work for the stop you just did or the next one: if there are both, the app asks which. So you can take a photo, or say *Gate locked*, without ticking first. Once you've ticked a stop the bar also has Undo, and the two buttons shrink to just their pictures.

## Stan
Stan is our little yellow robot, with a screen for a face, glowing blue eyes and his name on the black band across his front (new look, 14 Nov). He's the **Stan** button in the bottom bar of a run: tap him for directions to the next stop (that's the Driver Assistant). In the panel he tells you how he's helping: **Turn by turn** (blue lights on his face), **Pointing the way** (amber: he knows where the stop is, not the roads) or **Waiting for GPS** (grey). He talks when the voice is on, and pops up to cheer when you finish a run ("Stan-tastic!" is his word). **Got an idea? Tell Stan.** On the Runs page tap **Got an idea? Tell Stan** (or ⋯ → Tell Stan an idea in a run). Type what would make the app better, or what's bugging you, and tap **Send**. Stan answers, and your idea goes in the ideas book that Mike reads. You can use the microphone on the phone's keyboard to say it instead of typing.

**Stan has his own voice.** The first time, he asks to download it (about 100 MB, so do it on Wi-Fi: tap **Get it**). After that it loads by itself when you open the app and works with no signal. Until it's ready he uses the phone's voice. Prefer the phone's voice? **⚙ Settings → Voice → Phone's**. He also keeps you company when there's nothing to do (no runs yet, all done). Don't want him? **⚙ Settings → Stan → Hide**.


**Did you know… (Stan's cards).** Once a day, on the Runs page when you're off duty, Stan may show you one card. A **yellow** card is a tip (for example, your phone isn't recording where you tick, or a run keeps finishing after 6 am and would need a 15-minute earlier start). A **blue** card is just something interesting (how many papers you've delivered, your quickest morning). It's only ever about **your own** deliveries, and nobody else sees it. Tap **Got it** to put it away, **👍 More** if you like that kind, or **👎 Less** and he'll leave that kind alone for a month. Turning Stan off in Settings turns the cards off.

**Stan's Paper Run (a game).** When there are no runs to do and you're off duty, wait five seconds on the Runs page: Stan yawns and asks if you fancy a game. Tap **Play Stan's Paper Run**. **Throw** (yellow button) puts a paper in a letterbox; **Jump** gets Stan over dogs and locked gates. Don't throw while a sprinkler is spraying, and grab the green batteries or the phone dies. Three complaints and the run's over; finishing a street clears one. Your best score goes on the **family high scores**. It never shows while you're on duty, and turning Stan off in Settings turns it off.

## Changing the order of a run
If the sheet's order is wrong, **press and hold** a tile for half a second until it lifts, drag it, and let go. A blue line shows where it will go (left half of a tile = before it, right half = after). Hold a **street name** to move the whole street.
To move something a long way, keep holding it and drag it towards the **top or bottom of the screen**: the list scrolls by itself, faster the closer you are to the edge and the longer you hold it there. Move away from the edge to stop, then drop it in place.
**Everyone can have their own order.** When Lisa or Mum moves tiles, it's saved as **their own order** for that run, used every time *they* open it, and Mike's order (the usual one) isn't touched. The switch under the header then says **↕ My order** / **📄 Sheet order**. **⋯ → Use the usual order again** drops your own order. When Mike or Lisa (dispatchers) move tiles, they change the usual order everyone else sees, and only they have **⋯ → Put back in the sheet's order…**. If one of them wants an order the other can't move, **⋯ → Keep my own order…** keeps the order as it is now, or goes back to the last order they saved; **⋯ → Use the usual order again** switches back. The sheet's original order is always kept, and switching only changes what your phone shows.
Run 8 keeps its fixed building order and can't be rearranged.

## Your password
**⚙ Settings → Change password** on the Runs page. If you forget it, ask Mike for a temporary one.

## New version of the app
The app updates itself. If it says "A new version is ready", tap **Reload**. If something looks old, close the app fully and open it again.
