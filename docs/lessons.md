# Lessons

When something we made came out wrong, or didn't do what was meant, it is fixed twice: once in the thing itself, and once in **whatever let it through**. This page is the record of the second fix ([decision 0063](decisions/0063-lessons-loop.md)).

**How to add one** (in the same commit as the fix, newest at the top):

- **What happened** and **what was meant**, in a line or two, and how it was noticed.
- **What let it through**, one of: *the standard didn't say* · *the standard said, nothing checked* · *the check was there and was skipped*.
- **What changed in the standard**, in this order of preference: a **check** (a test or a rule the publish checks enforce), then a **rule** (CONTRIBUTING.md, a decision, a business rule), then a **note** for whoever does this next. Or **nothing, because…** if it's a one-off not worth a rule.

A fix that needs no lesson says `[no-lesson]` in its commit message. Lessons are never deleted; if a later one replaces an earlier one, say so in the new one.
